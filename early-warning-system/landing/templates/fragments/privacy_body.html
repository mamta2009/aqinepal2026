Privacy Policy

Climate Compass (Nepal air quality & respiratory preparedness)
Operated by intellADAPT / Edwise Tech Company

This Privacy Policy applies to the Climate Compass website and mobile application (including services hosted at https://ews.intelladapt.ai). It explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it.

1. Introduction

intellADAPT ("we," "our," or "us") provides the Climate Compass to help communities and enrolled facilities receive environmental situational awareness related to air quality, heat, and preparedness alerts. This is an informational and preparedness tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnoses, treatment, or official government health guidance.

By creating an account, registering for alerts, or using the app or website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect:

  • Account and contact details: name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number (if provided), password (stored as a secure hash), city/municipality enrolment, and facility or workplace names you associate with your account.
  • Preferences and consent: alert channel preferences (SMS, WhatsApp, email), consent timestamps, and related verification status.
  • Alert and notification records: messages we send you, delivery/status metadata from messaging providers, and in-app notification inbox entries.
  • Facility preparedness actions: optional action logs you submit when signed in (for example preparedness steps linked to a facility site).
  • Friends and family alert contacts: names and contact details you choose to save for optional user-initiated alerts.
  • Device and technical information: IP address, device/app version, browser type, and basic usage logs needed to operate and secure the service.
  • Environmental data: air quality, weather, and heat information from third-party providers. This is location/city-based operational data, not medical records.

We do not collect payment card information through this app.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and manage your registration or account
  • Verify contact channels and authenticate sign-in
  • Send environmental and preparedness alerts you opted into
  • Support facility preparedness features for approved enrollees
  • Operate dashboards, APIs, and notification delivery
  • Improve reliability, security, and support
  • Comply with legal obligations

Optional AI-assisted help features (such as explanatory chat grounded in public guides) may process questions you submit for that purpose. AI outputs are informational only and are not medical or professional advice.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with trusted service providers that help us operate the platform, such as:

  • Cloud hosting and databases
  • Email delivery providers
  • SMS / WhatsApp messaging providers (for example Twilio)
  • Air quality / weather data providers

These providers process data on our behalf and are expected to protect it under applicable agreements and law. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal data, including:

  • Encrypted transport (HTTPS) for the public website and API
  • Hashed passwords
  • Access controls for operator/admin tools
  • Monitoring and operational safeguards

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal account data (including name, email, phone/WhatsApp numbers, facility enrolment details, preferences, verification records, and notification history) for as long as your account remains active and the data is needed to provide Climate Compass alert and preparedness services.

If you delete your account, we permanently delete or anonymize associated personal data within 30 days, except where we must retain limited information to comply with law, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, or meet security and audit requirements.

Facility preparedness action logs may be retained in anonymized form after account deletion so that personal identifiers (such as contact id and email hash) are removed while non-personal operational history can remain for programme continuity.

Short-lived verification and account-deletion codes are stored only for the time needed to complete verification (typically minutes) and are then no longer usable.

Server logs and security records may be retained for a limited period for troubleshooting and abuse prevention, then deleted or aggregated.

7. Data Deletion Process

You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time.

How to submit a deletion request:

  • In the mobile app: open Account and use Delete account.
  • On the web (no app sign-in required): visit https://ews.intelladapt.ai/delete-account and follow the email confirmation steps.
  • By email: write to info@intelladapt.com with subject Data Deletion Request, and include your full name and registered email address.

After we verify your identity, we will:

  • Delete your contact/account record
  • Remove related personal consent and notification records where applicable
  • Remove shared-alert contacts and related personal dispatch logs associated with your account
  • Anonymize facility action logs so they are no longer linked to you

Verified deletion requests are generally completed within 30 days. Some limited information may be retained where required by law or for fraud prevention and security.

8. Children's Privacy

The Climate Compass is intended for adults and professional or community enrollee use. It is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

9. Cookies and Analytics

The website may use cookies or similar technologies as needed for secure sessions, basic preferences, and service operation. You can control cookies through your browser settings; some features may not work if cookies are disabled.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, use the deletion options above or email info@intelladapt.com.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Material updates will be posted on this page. Continued use of the service after an update means you accept the revised policy.
