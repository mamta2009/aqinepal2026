Delete your account

Request permanent deletion of your Climate Compass registration and associated personal data. This page is available without installing the mobile app (Google Play / App Store account-deletion requirement).

What gets deleted

  • Your contact / registration record (name, email, phone, preferences)
  • Consent records tied to your account
  • Notification delivery history addressed to you
  • Friends & family alert contacts you saved

Facility preparedness action history may be retained in anonymized form (without your contact id or email) for operational audit.

Deletion is processed immediately after you confirm with a one-time code. The same email can register again afterward.

This cannot be undone. Type DELETE exactly when confirming.
