# School air-quality action guide

**A planning aid for teachers and school administrators**

Use Climate Compass with your school’s policies and official local guidance. Assign one staff member to check conditions before outdoor activities.

## Good

- Normal assembly, recess, and sports are generally suitable
- Keep normal support available for children with individual health plans

## Moderate

- Watch students who are sensitive to air pollution
- Offer extra breaks and water
- Reduce very strenuous activity if children report symptoms

## Use extra care

- Consider shorter recess and outdoor lessons
- Move strenuous sports indoors or reduce intensity
- Keep sensitive students indoors where practical
- Review window use based on outdoor air and indoor comfort

## Unhealthy

- Move assembly, sports, and strenuous activities indoors where possible
- Limit time waiting outdoors for buses or pickup
- Contact families according to school policy
- Follow official instructions if local authorities issue an alert

## Daily staff check

- [ ] Nearest city selected
- [ ] Air status recorded
- [ ] Heat status checked
- [ ] Assembly decision shared
- [ ] Sports and recess decision shared
- [ ] Sensitive-student support reviewed
- [ ] Conditions checked again if smoke, dust, heat, or weather changes

Climate Compass is an educational demonstration. It does not replace official closure decisions, emergency alerts, school health plans, or medical advice.
