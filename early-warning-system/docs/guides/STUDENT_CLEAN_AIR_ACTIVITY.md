# Become an air detective

**For students ages 10 and up · 20–30 minutes**

Air pollution is not always easy to see. In this activity, you will observe your surroundings and compare what you notice with the air-quality information in Climate Compass.

## What you need

- A notebook or sheet of paper
- A pencil
- Access to the Climate Compass dashboard with an adult or teacher

## Step 1: Look outside

From a safe place, write down:

- Is the sky clear, hazy, smoky, or dusty?
- Can you smell smoke or exhaust?
- Is traffic light, medium, or heavy?
- Is it windy, still, hot, or rainy?

## Step 2: Check your city

Open `/dashboard` and choose the city closest to you. Record:

- The plain-language air status
- The advice for students
- The AQI or PM2.5 number from **See the numbers**

## Step 3: Compare

Did the air-quality status match what you could see or smell? Remember: tiny particles can be present even when the air looks clear.

## Step 4: Take one action

Choose one helpful action:

- Walk or cycle when it is safe instead of using a vehicle
- Do not burn rubbish
- Save electricity
- Care for plants and trees
- Share today’s air-quality advice with your family

## Think about it

1. Why can air pollution be difficult to notice?
2. Who may need extra care on a polluted day?
3. What could your school do to reduce pollution?

Climate Compass is an educational tool, not medical advice. Ask a trusted adult or health professional if you feel unwell.
