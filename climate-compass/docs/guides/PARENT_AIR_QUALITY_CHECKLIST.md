# Family air-quality checklist

**A quick guide for parents and caregivers**

## Before children go outside

- Check the plain-language status for your nearest city
- Read the parent and sensitive-child recommendations
- Consider both air quality and heat
- Check for smoke, dust, or strong traffic pollution nearby

## When the air needs extra care

- Choose gentler or shorter outdoor play
- Take more breaks
- Keep needed inhalers or prescribed medicines nearby
- Watch for coughing, wheezing, unusual tiredness, or breathing difficulty
- Move activity indoors if conditions worsen

## At home

- Keep windows closed when outdoor air is poor, if the room can stay safely ventilated
- Avoid smoking or burning rubbish
- Reduce indoor dust and strong fumes
- Encourage water breaks during hot weather

## Important

Children with asthma, heart or lung conditions, and very young children may be more sensitive to pollution. Follow advice from your child’s health professional.

Climate Compass provides educational guidance, not a diagnosis, treatment plan, or emergency warning. Follow local authorities and seek medical help when needed.
