# A classroom lesson on clean air

**For teachers · Grades 6–8 · 35–45 minutes**

## Learning goals

Students will be able to:

- Explain AQI in plain language
- Describe why PM2.5 can affect breathing
- Read a daily air-quality status
- Suggest actions that reduce exposure and pollution

## Warm-up

Ask: **Can air be polluted even when it looks clean?** Invite two or three short answers.

Explain that PM2.5 particles are much smaller than a grain of sand. We may not see them, but monitoring stations and weather services can measure them.

## Explore Climate Compass

1. Open `/dashboard`.
2. Select your nearest city.
3. Read the plain-language status before opening the technical details.
4. Compare the school and student recommendations.
5. Open **See the numbers** and explain the AQI and PM2.5 tooltips.

## Group activity

Give each group one school situation:

- Morning assembly
- Outdoor sports
- Recess
- Waiting for the school bus
- Opening classroom windows

Ask the group to make a plan for a **Good**, **Moderate**, and **Unhealthy** day. Plans should include children with asthma or other breathing sensitivities.

## Exit question

Write one sentence: **Today’s air status matters because…**

## Teacher note

Climate Compass may use live air and heat data when providers are configured. Some charts and health-case examples are illustrative. It is not medical advice or an official school-closure system. Follow local authority and school policies.
